Previous Q&A:

Q1: Why am I tokenizing the data?
A1: Tokenization is essential because:
    - BERT models can't process raw text directly
    - It converts text into numerical sequences that the model can understand
    - Example:
        Text: "Unauthorized login attempt detected"
        Tokens: ["un", "##author", "##ized", "login", "attempt", "detected"]
        Numbers: [2297, 2571, 2182, 2523, 3290, 2034]
    - Adds special tokens like [CLS] and [SEP] that BERT needs

Training Parameters Deep Dive:
---------------------------
warmup_steps=500 Explained:
1. Purpose:
   - Gradually increases the learning rate from 0 to the specified maximum
   - Helps prevent training instability in early stages
   - Particularly important when fine-tuning BERT models

2. How it works:
   ```
   Learning Rate
   ^
   |     _________ (max learning rate)
   |    /
   |   /
   |  /
   | /
   |/
   +----------------> Training Steps
   0   500
   ```
   - First 500 steps: Learning rate increases linearly
   - After 500 steps: Learning rate stays at maximum value

3. Why it's important:
   - Large learning rates early in training can cause:
     * Unstable gradients
     * Poor weight updates
     * Model failing to learn
   - Warmup provides a "gentle start" to training
   - Especially crucial when fine-tuning pre-trained models like BERT

4. In our code:
   ```python
   training_args = TrainingArguments(
       warmup_steps=500,  # First 500 steps use increasing learning rate
       # ... other arguments
   )
   ```

Gradient Norm Explained:
---------------------
1. What is Gradient Norm?
   - A single number measuring the magnitude of gradients
   - Calculated as the square root of sum of squared gradients
   - Helps monitor training stability

2. Why it's Important:
   ```
   Too High (> 10):
   - Indicates unstable training
   - Risk of "exploding gradients"
   - Model might fail to learn

   Too Low (< 0.1):
   - Might indicate "vanishing gradients"
   - Learning could be too slow
   - Model might be stuck

   Good Range (0.5 - 5):
   - Stable learning
   - Effective weight updates
   - Healthy training process
   ```
